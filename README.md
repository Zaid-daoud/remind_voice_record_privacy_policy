# Privacy Policy for Remind

**Last updated:** February 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the **Remind** mobile application (the "Application" or "Service").

By using the Application, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. No User Accounts Required

The Application does **not** require or support user account creation, registration, passwords, or personal login profiles. You can use all features of the Application without providing an email address, phone number, or personal identity.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### A. Location Data
With your explicit permission, the Application accesses your device's precise and approximate location (GPS and network-based location) solely to:
- Calculate accurate Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) based on your geographic coordinates.
- Determine the precise Qiblah direction toward the Kaaba in Mecca.
- Display nearby mosques and calculate their distance from your current location.

Location data is processed ephemerally on your device. We do not track, store, or profile your location history on our servers.

### B. Audio / Microphone Access
The Application may request microphone access if you choose to use built-in voice commands or offline speech recognition features. Audio processing is conducted locally on your device using on-device models. Voice recordings are never recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted off your device.

### C. Crash Diagnostics and App Performance
We use **Google Firebase Crashlytics** to automatically collect anonymized diagnostic data when the Application encounters a crash or error. This information includes device model, operating system version, and anonymized stack traces. It is used strictly to identify and fix software bugs to improve stability.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Application integrates trusted third-party services that may process anonymized data in compliance with their privacy policies:
- **Google Firebase Crashlytics:** Anonymized crash reporting and stability diagnostics. ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim:** Used to display interactive map data for nearby mosques. ([OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy))

---

## 4. Permissions Used by the Application

The Application requests the following device permissions only when necessary for specific features:
- **LOCATION (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):** Required for prayer times, Qiblah direction, and nearby mosques.
- **MICROPHONE (RECORD_AUDIO):** Used optionally for on-device voice recognition.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS & FOREGROUND_SERVICE:** Required to display daily Adhan reminders and the user-visible persistent prayer countdown timer notification.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Security

Because We do not collect personal identities or user accounts, We do not maintain personal user databases. App settings and preferences (such as selected prayer calculation methods or favorite Hadith) are stored locally on your device memory. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling the Application.

All network communications between the Application and external APIs (such as mapping services) use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Application is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.
